# Outreach scripts — bakery & pâtisserie prospects

Three rules before anything else:

1. **One personalised message per shop**, sent by a human, from a real address. No bcc blasts, no scraped forms.
2. **Reference something true** (the award, the new address, the Saturday-only loaf). If you can't, you're not ready to email them.
3. **Never say their site is bad.** Say what is checkable: *single language*, *no online ordering*, *menu only on a chalkboard*. Cold mail that insults the previous designer loses the client and the referral.
4. **Payment talk comes third, not first.** No crypto word in a cold email; USDT appears on the *quote*, as an
   alternative the client agrees to, with the amount still written in euros. Full rule: `sales/PAYMENTS-USDT.md`.

commercial email even B2B — **for French leads, phone or in-person beats inbox.**

## 7. Proposal skeleton (one page, always)

1. **What we heard** — three sentences quoting *their* words ("you said the Saturday loaf sells out by 9").
2. **What it will do** — five bullets, verbs first, no adjectives.
3. **Three options** — with the middle one marked *recommended*.
4. **What we need from you** — photos, prices, hours, logo, the texts in their language. Deadline per item.
5. **When** — start date, 7 working days, launch date written next to "your press moment".
6. **Not included** — payments, ads, social, photography beyond the pass, content after launch.
7. **Sign** — 40 % deposit, IBAN, this page is valid 21 days.
