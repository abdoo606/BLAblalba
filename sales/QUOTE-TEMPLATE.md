## Rules I applied when writing these

- The **price is read from `sales/drive-clients-pricing.csv`** — never retyped from memory.
- Never a "limited slots" line. Never "your competitors". Never an adjective about their current site.
- Every claim about their web presence must have been opened by me or you on the day it's written.
- The VAT sentence is not decoration: it's what stops an invoice becoming an argument.
- If they ask for a discount: max 15 %, and only against a case study, a hard deadline, or a paid-in-full year.
