## EN — Privacy notice (business contacts)

I'm **Abdulrhman Thali**, freelance web developer, Naaldwijk, the Netherlands.

**What I collect:** business name, city, professional contact details (public email, phone, address), and notes
about a shop's public web presence — menu, opening hours, languages, whether pre-orders exist.

**Where it comes from:** public sources only — the shop's own website, its Google Business listing, its public
Instagram, and press or award coverage. I do not buy lists and I do not use scraped personal addresses.

**Why, and on what legal basis:** one purpose — to offer my web services to that business. Basis: **legitimate
interest** (GDPR art. 6.1.f) for B2B direct marketing; in France this also follows art. L.34-5 of the CPCE
(consumer consent isn't required for business-to-business email, provided the message is relevant to the
recipient's professional activity, the sender is clearly identified and objecting is free and easy).

**Who else sees it:** nobody. No marketing platform, no CRM vendor beyond Google Workspace, no resale.

**How long:** 3 years from the last contact from you (CNIL's reference period for prospects), then deleted.
If you object, your address goes on a permanent suppression list — kept only so I don't contact you again.

**Your rights:** access, correction, deletion, objection, and a complaint to your supervisory authority
(Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens — France: CNIL — Spain: AEPD — UK: ICO). Write to abdulrhmant948@gmail.com and I
act within 30 days.

**If you become a client:** the data I hold about your customers (pre-order names and phone numbers) is passed
straight to you; I keep no order database, and I process it as your sub-processor only for as long as the site
needs it.
