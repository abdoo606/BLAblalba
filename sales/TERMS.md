# Terms for every Levain Kit project — 1 page, attach to the deposit invoice

Drafted 2026-10-11 for **Abdulrhman Thali, freelance web developer, Homs, Syria** (a sole independent with no EU
establishment). Written to be readable by a baker: 8 clauses, no small print games. Not a substitute for a lawyer if a job goes above ~€5,000.

**1. What I do.** I design, build and publish a website for [BUSINESS] using my own Levain Kit, in the languages
and with the options listed in quote [YYYY-00X]. Content (texts, prices, photos, opening hours) is provided by
you; I format it and enter it.

**2. What I don't do.** I do not run ads, do not manage social media, do not guarantee a Google ranking, do not
handle deliveries or payments between you and your customers, and I am not a reseller of hosting or domains.
Any sentence like "you'll be first on Google" is not part of this agreement — nobody honest can promise it.

**3. Money.** 40 % on signature, 60 % on go-live, per the invoice. Payment within 14 days. Late: statutory B2B
interest + €40 fixed recovery cost (Directive 2011/7/EU). Work pauses while an invoice is more than 14 days
overdue, and restarts when it's paid — that's fairer than a lawyer's letter for both of us.

**4. Your property, from day one.** The domain is registered **in your name, on your email, with your 2FA**, and
so is hosting. Where I pay for something on your behalf, it is billed at cost + €25 admin, and the registrar
account is transferred to you within 7 days with a written note of the login, the expiry date and the support
channel. The website files live in a Git repository you own (or that I hand over to you in a zip on delivery).
You can leave with everything at any time, without asking me.

**5. Revisions.** Two rounds included, each round = one consolidated list of changes (not a change per day).
More rounds: 90 € each. Content you send late pushes the deadline by the same number of days — the only part of
the schedule that ever moves.

**6. Data about your customers.** If pre-orders run through the site, orders are delivered straight to your
WhatsApp/email; I keep no order database, I don't add tracking pixels, and I don't put analytics cookies
up without a consent banner. When I need access to your Google/business accounts to publish, I use the least
access possible and I hand back the password after go-live. If a legal request for the personal data of your
customers ever lands on me, I forward it to you and do nothing else.

**7. Liability & insurance.** My total liability for a project is capped at the fees you paid for that project
in the last 6 months. I'm not liable for lost sales, a bad Google review, a bakery-day lost to something on
your side, or a third-party platform's decisions (Stripe pausing an account is not my breach). I carry [insurer,
policy n°] professional indemnity. You keep backups of your own content and are responsible for the accuracy of
prices, allergen statements and legal notices on your site (that part is yours in every EU country — allergen
labelling in particular).

**8. Ending it.** Either side can stop with 7 days' written notice. If you stop: the deposit is not refunded
(it's bought the time already reserved), you get everything built so far in a zip, and I remove my name from
any public reference. If I stop: you get a full refund of what you paid minus the work delivered, priced at
hourly, plus every file. No hostage-taking — that's the whole difference between a freelancer and a lock-in.

*Confidentiality:* what I learn about your volumes, your supplier prices and your margin never leaves this
project. *Contact:* abdulrhmant948@gmail.com · [phone]. *Disputes:* the courts of the defendant's place of residence, after one honest phone call. *Governing law:*
Syrian law (or the law of the entity that signs, if that changes). Realistically: with a 40–50 % deposit and
two revisions written down, nobody sues over a €1,450 bakery site — the deposit is the enforcement, not the clause.
