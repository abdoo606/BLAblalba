## How to quote (rules of the studio)

1. **Never lead with the price.** Lead with the two sentences in `sales/OUTREACH.md`, price arrives in writing.
2. **Always three options.** Send Baguette / Croissant / Pâtisserie in every proposal, with Croissant pre-checked. Middle-option anchoring is why 60 % land on €1,450.
3. **Deposit 40 %** before work starts, balance at go-live, nothing ships before the balance clears.
4. **Annual care is sold at handover**, not at the start — “€45/month, cancel any time” converts far better once the site is live and they've seen the countdown widget work.
5. **Discounts:** 15 % max, only for (a) a real launch deadline, (b) a logo + name they let us show as a case study, (c) three months of care paid upfront. Log it in the CSV as `discount_pct`.
6. **Never discount the care plan.** Swap it for a free extra language instead — the recurring revenue is worth more than the one-off.
7. **Non-profits, village bakeries under 2 staff:** the “village rate” = Baguette at €590. Two per year, max.
8. **Currency:** EU clients in EUR, UK clients in GBP (~£0.86/€), US/CA in USD. Round to a clean number in their currency: €1,450 → $1,590 → £1,250.

- “Fully custom design from scratch” (we have three systems we know are accessible and fast)
- WordPress / Shopify hand-holding beyond embedding links and menu handoff
- Paid ads, social media management, photography beyond the half-day pass
