## Suggested dial order (first 10 working days)

**Week 1 — the seven A-leads.** Nothing to lose, they have no site to defend:
- 1. Northern Rye (Newcastle / Tyneside) — 1,017 € · Instagram-only per public listings
- 2. Boulangerie-Pâtisserie Familha (Chambonas, Ardèche) — 4,060 € · Zero national web funnel
- 3. Le Four à Pattes (Mazaugues, Var) — 1,017 € · Unknown - verify
- 4. Forn Pa Serra (Barcelona) — 1,148 € · NO website at all - hottest lead in the list
- 5. Handy Bakery (Barcelona (Gràcia)) — 2,830 € · Brand new; likely no site yet
- 6. Wild Crumb (Bozeman, MT) — 4,060 € · Has a site; no ordering, no bilingual
- 7. Weltons Tiny Bakeshop (Charleston, SC) — 1,215 € · Instagram-first; national press with no funnel

**Week 2 — B-leads with a deadline hook.** TV-award winners and new openings have a 4–8 week window
where the press coverage still converts. Call them in this order: M6/James Beard winners first
(Familha, Entremets et Créations, Boulangerie Bleue, Wild Crumb, JinJu, Pancho Anaya), then the
multi-site groups (Bread Source, Pettigrew, Belle's, Grall's) where the Pâtisserie tier is realistic.

**Pipeline math, conservatively:** 36 leads → 5 % cold-email reply ≈ 2 replies, 30 % call-to-quote ≈ 11
quotes, 35 % quote-to-close ≈ **4 signed jobs ≈ 6,900 € one-off + ~180 €/month recurring.** A second
100-row list (directories, not awards) is what actually fills a quarter: awards lists are the *easiest*
to pitch but the smallest pool.

## Rules we follow on this list

- One email per business, no mass bcc, no scraped contact forms with 40 messages a day.
- If they reply "not interested", it is closed and logged, never chased.
- Prices quoted from `docs/PRICING.md`; no invented discounts at the phone stage.
- A shop's award is *their* goodwill — we reference it, we never put it in an ad for our studio.
